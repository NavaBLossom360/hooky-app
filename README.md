# Hooky

Catch someone new. Swipe, match, chat, and go live. Not a dating app.

The name works both ways: a hook you catch someone with, and playing hooky.
In the app a like is a **hook**, a match is a **catch**, being online is
**playing hooky**, and the paid tier is the **Hall Pass**.

Hooky is a swipe-to-make-friends app for people 13 and up, with live video calls
between matches who are online at the same time. Users only ever see
other users in their own age group, and every safety rule is enforced twice: once
in the client for fast feedback, and once in the database where it can't be bypassed.

## Run it

There is no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with
any static server. With no `config.js` present the app runs in **demo mode**:
sixteen fake users, everything stored in `localStorage`, fake chat replies.

To connect a real backend:

1. Create a Supabase project and run `supabase/schema.sql` in the SQL editor.
2. Turn on Email OTP under Authentication.
3. Copy `config.example.js` to `config.js` and fill in the URL and anon key.

## Safety design

- Teens only see and are seen by their own age group. Enforced by RLS.
- Selfie age check at signup. Simulated in the demo. A real launch needs an
  third-party age-estimation vendor, and unverified profiles never enter Discover.
- Chats involving a minor block phone numbers, social handles, other app names,
  links, and meet-up or photo requests. Adults get a warning instead.
- Location is region only. No last name, no school, no last-seen.
- Report and block from every card and every chat. Reports are private.
- Safety tips screen with sextortion guidance and hotline info.
- Photos must pass a moderation classifier before going live (see notes at the
  bottom of `schema.sql`).

## Before a real launch

- Age estimation vendor integration (edge function sets `verification`).
- Photo moderation pipeline and human review queue for reports.
- App store billing webhook that writes `premium_until`.
- Privacy policy, terms, and COPPA / state teen-privacy law review.
- Push notifications and a native wrapper (Capacitor works well with this codebase).

## Live calls and presence

Everyone with the app open is tracked on a presence channel. In Discover, online
people show a "Playing hooky" badge and sort first, and there is an online-only
filter. Two people who have caught each other and are both online can start a
video call from the chat, the match screen, or the Chats tab. The other person
has to accept. Calls run over WebRTC with signaling on a per-match realtime
channel, nothing is recorded, and either side can end or report from the call
screen. In demo mode the call shows your own camera and a placeholder for the
other person.

## Install it like an app

Hooky ships a web app manifest and a service worker, so on a phone you can add
it to the home screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon. It also works
offline for the shell. To ship to app stores, wrap this folder with Capacitor.

## Shipping to the App Store and Google Play

A web app can't be submitted directly, so Hooky ships as a native wrapper around
this folder using Capacitor. What's already here:

- `capacitor.config.json` with the app id, name, colors, and splash settings.
- `build.ps1`, which copies the shippable files into `www/` for Capacitor.
- Store-size icons in `assets/` (1024 for the store listing, 512 and 192 for
  Android and the manifest, 180 for iOS home screen), generated by `logo.html`.
- `legal.html` with Community Guidelines, Terms, and a Privacy Policy template.
  Host it at a public URL; both stores require a live Privacy Policy link.
- In-app report, block, filtering, account deletion, and a support contact,
  which Apple requires for any app with user-generated content.
- No links to outside payment; Hooky+ is described as an app store subscription.

Steps, on a Mac with Xcode for iOS (Android works from Windows):

1. Change `SUPPORT_EMAIL` in `app.js` and the app id in `capacitor.config.json`.
2. Create `config.js` with your Supabase project so the build is not in demo mode.
3. Run `build.ps1`, then `npm i @capacitor/core @capacitor/cli @capacitor/ios @capacitor/android`,
   `npx cap add ios`, `npx cap add android`, `npx cap sync`.
4. iOS: in Xcode set NSCameraUsageDescription and NSMicrophoneUsageDescription
   ("Hooky uses your camera and microphone for live calls and the selfie age check").
   Android: camera, microphone, and internet permissions in the manifest.
5. Add in-app purchases for Hooky+ with StoreKit / Google Play Billing and a
   webhook that writes `premium_until`. Stores reject subscriptions that bypass them.
6. Wire the selfie step to an age-estimation vendor and photo uploads to a
   moderation classifier before submission. Reviewers test both.
7. App Store Connect: age rating 12+ (infrequent mild mature themes), category
   Social Networking, Privacy Policy URL, and a demo account for the reviewer.
   Google Play: Teen rating, Data safety form, and the Child Safety Standards
   policy declaration, which is mandatory for social apps.

Regenerate the icons any time by opening `logo.html` on the dev server and
clicking "Download all PNGs".

## Test it on your phone

The app is live at **https://navablossom360.github.io/hooky-app/** (GitHub Pages,
served over HTTPS from the `main` branch). Anything pushed to `main` appears
there within a minute or so.

Open that link on your phone, then install it so it behaves like a real app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the three-dot menu, then "Install app" or
  "Add to Home screen".

It then launches full-screen with the Hooky icon, no browser bar.

HTTPS matters here: the camera, the microphone, and the service worker all
refuse to run over plain `http://`, so testing over a local network IP will not
let you try the selfie step or a live call. Use the Pages URL.

`qr.html` renders a scannable QR card for the Pages URL. Open it on the dev
server and call `upload()` from the console to save it to `assets/`.

**What works in this build:** everything except a real second person. The app is
in demo mode with seeded profiles, so live calls show your own camera with a
placeholder for the other person. Point `config.js` at a Supabase project to get
real accounts, real presence, and real peer-to-peer calls.
