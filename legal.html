Community Guidelines

Hooky is for making friends. These rules keep it that way. Breaking them gets content removed and can get you banned.

Be real

Use your real first name or a nickname, your real age, and photos of yourself. No impersonating anyone. No pretending to be a different age.

Be kind

No bullying, hate, threats, or harassment. No sexual content, nudity, or sexual requests of any kind. This is strictly enforced and reported to authorities where required by law.

Keep it on Hooky

Don't pressure people to leave the app or share phone numbers, addresses, or other contact info. Don't ask for photos. Don't try to meet up with someone you only know from here.

Live calls

Face on, keep it appropriate. Anyone can hang up at any time for any reason. Calls are not recorded by Hooky. Recording or screenshotting someone without consent is against the rules.

Report anything

Every profile, chat, and call has a report button. Reports are private. We review all reports and act on safety reports within 24 hours.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account: email, display name, birthdate, region (state or country), bio, interests, and any photo you add.
  • Age check: before you sign up, a short camera scan estimates your age. It runs entirely on your own device: no photo or video is sent to Hooky or to anyone else, and no faceprint or other biometric identifier is created or stored. The only thing we keep is the result, an estimated age, which is saved with your account and compared with the birthday you enter.
  • Activity: swipes, matches, messages, reports, blocks, and whether the app is open (online status). Live calls are not recorded.
  • Device: device type, app version, crash logs, and IP address for security.

What we don't collect

No precise location. No contacts. No advertising identifiers. We do not sell personal information.

How we use it

To run the app, show you people in your age group, deliver messages and calls, keep users safe, respond to reports, process subscriptions, and comply with the law.

Who sees it

Other users in your age group see your name, age, region, bio, interests, and photo. Service providers (hosting, photo moderation, payments) process data on our behalf. The age check has no provider: it runs on your device. We disclose data to authorities when required by law or to protect a child's safety.

Kids and teens

Hooky is not for children under 13. If we learn a user is under 13 we delete the account. Teen accounts have stricter messaging filters and are never shown to adults, with one exception: 17 and 18 year olds can see each other, and no other minor and adult ever can.

Your choices

Edit your profile any time. Delete your account from the Me tab, which deletes your data within 30 days except where we must keep it for safety or legal reasons. Contact [support email] to exercise privacy rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws.

Retention and security

Data is kept while your account is active. Messages are encrypted in transit. Report records may be kept after deletion to prevent abuse.
